## Errors

These contradict another part of the Doc, the brief, or the book.

**E1. Business case overstates the push saving.** Assumption 18 says 80% of guardians have a smartphone and 70% *of those* activate the app, which is 56% of guardians. The business case applies 70% of *all* guardians, so SMS cost left is 13,800,000 VND a year. At 56% it is 20,300,000 VND. Net recurring benefit falls from 90,600,000 to 84,100,000 VND a year, and cumulative net becomes year 1 -255.9M, year 5 -29.9M, year 6 +26.6M: payback moves from the fifth year to the sixth. Affects 2A business case tables, form page 1 Project Purpose ("90,600,000", "fifth year"), and RTM BR05. Team decision: either restate assumption 18 as 70% of all guardians, or recompute.

**E2. The "part-time" mobile developer is loaded above full time.** The charter gives 2.5 person-months (400 hours) "between M2 and M5", which is 9 weeks, or 44 hours a week. The dictionary puts 336 of MOB1's 400 hours between 19 October and 11 December (7.6 weeks, 44 hours a week). The charter says the mobile developer "leaves after M5", but 1.9.1.2 gives MOB1 64 hours running to 5 January 2027. Choose one: full time for the window, a longer window, or fewer hours.
