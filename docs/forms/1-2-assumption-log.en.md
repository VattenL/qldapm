# 1.2 Assumption Log

*Form 1.2, A Project Manager's Book of Forms, 3rd edition, PDF page 22 (printed page 11), filled as the assumption log that PMBOK 6 section 4.1.3.2, printed page 81 (PDF page 120), makes an output of Develop Project Charter. Assumptions 1 to 19 are the assumptions of section 1.6 of the charter, as committed on 16 September 2026 and amended on 14 November 2026, copied word for word with their numbers, so that "assumption N" means the same row everywhere. Assumption 20 holds the stakeholder readings of forms 1.3 and 1.4, which PMBOK 6 section 13.1.3.4, printed page 515 (PDF page 554), says belong in this log. C1 to C6 are the six constraints of section 1.5 of the charter, copied word for word, because the book documents constraints in the same log (PDF page 20, printed page 9). The highlighted readings of the scope package and of forms 2.23, 2.28, and 2.32 stay in those forms and are not entered here.*

*Category is the preparer's grouping. Responsible Party names the role that validates the assumption, using the role names of form 2.32; Due Date is the milestone by which it must be validated, with the milestone's baselined date; both are the preparer's reading and are highlighted. Actions are copied from the charter's risk responses and approval requirements where the charter states them, and are otherwise the preparer's proposals, highlighted. A constraint is not validated, so its Due Date is empty and its Actions say how it is held. Status is Active for every row, because no project document records an assumption as validated; where a Due Date is earlier than the Date Prepared, the validation is overdue and the team should record its result.*
