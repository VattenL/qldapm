*Form 2.29, A Project Manager's Book of Forms, 3rd edition, PDF page 128 (printed page 117); PMBOK 6 section 9.2.3.3, printed pages 326 to 327 (PDF pages 365 to 366). Built from the charter as committed on 16 September 2026, the scope package as committed on 14 October 2026, and the cost estimate of 8 October 2026, form 2.20, and kept in step with form 2.28, which holds the skills, the specifications, the hours, the amounts, and the basis. Like form 2.28, it applies the reduced scope of form 2.20, so the mobile developer, the test phones, the store accounts, the external data-entry support, and the team workspace are gone. The third level of People holds the resource codes with the work each one leads; the skill level, 1 to 2 years, is the one form 2.20 costs every role at. A highlighted quantity is an assumption.*
