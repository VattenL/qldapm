# 2.32 Risk Register

*Form 2.32, A Project Manager's Book of Forms, 3rd edition, PDF page 141 (printed page 130), filled as the output of PMBOK 6 section 11.2.3.1, printed page 417 (PDF page 456). The table prints in two banks, one above the other; row N of the second table continues row N of the first, and the ID column of the second bank, which the printed form does not carry, was added at the review of 5 November 2026 so that each bank can be read on its own. Each risk statement follows the structure of Table 2.29 of the book, "If CONDITION exists, EVENT may occur, leading to EFFECT". R1 to R12 restate, in that structure, the risks of the Overall project risk table in Part 2A of the charter as committed on 16 September 2026; the charter keeps its short descriptions, and its Type column, which the form does not print, is written in Comments. R13 to R18 are not in the charter: R13 to R16 come from assumption and constraint analysis and document analysis (PMBOK 6 section 11.2.2, printed pages 414 to 416 (PDF pages 453 to 455)), and R17 and R18 restate the two project risks the pending scope change request of 8 October 2026 adds.*

*<mark>Highlighted</mark> cells are the preparer's reading and are to be confirmed by the team. Probability and Impact use the three levels defined under Probability and impact scales above the form; each Impact cell gives the level and the effect on that objective. Score is written Probability-Impact, taking the highest of the four Impact levels, the relative scoring of Book of Forms form 2.32, PDF page 140 (printed page 129); its priority is read from the matrix. Owner names one role per risk, the person who manages and tracks it (PMBOK 6 section 11.3.2.7, printed page 426); roles are named as in form 2.28 Resource Requirements, with the label in brackets. Response begins with the strategy for threats of PMBOK 6 section 11.5.2.4, printed page 442, which is the preparer's reading. Actions are written as "work package code and title: what is done", one per work package, separated by semicolons, and Responsible Party is the Responsible Person of those work packages in the WBS dictionary.*

*Revised Probability, Impact, and Score forecast the risk expected to remain once the response is carried out, the residual risk of PMBOK 6 section 11.5.3.3, printed page 448, so each of them is highlighted. Status is Open for every risk because no document records a response as complete. Two change requests (form 3.3) are pending: the schedule change request of 30 September 2026 lists R8 and R9 as updated, and the scope change request of 8 October 2026 raises R6 and defers F12. Every risk follows the charter until the sponsor decides; Comments say what each request would change. Cells changed in version 1.1 are wrapped in an ins tag.*
