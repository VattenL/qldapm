# 2.32 Risk Register

*Form 2.32, A Project Manager's Book of Forms, 3rd edition, PDF page 141 (printed page 130), filled as the output of PMBOK 6 section 11.2.3.1, printed page 417 (PDF page 456). The table prints in two banks, one above the other; row N of the second table continues row N of the first. R1 to R12 are copied as written from the Overall project risk table in Part 2A of the charter, built from the charter as committed on 16 September 2026; the charter's Type column, which the form does not print, is written in Comments. R13 to R18 are not in the charter, so each of their cells is highlighted: R13 to R16 come from assumption and constraint analysis and document analysis (PMBOK 6 section 11.2.2, printed pages 414 to 416 (PDF pages 453 to 455)), and R17 and R18 are the two project risks the pending scope change request of 8 October 2026 adds, copied as written.*

*<mark>Highlighted</mark> cells are the preparer's first reading and are to be confirmed by the team. Probability and Impact use a relative High, Medium, Low scale read from the charter's own wording, because no form 2.31 defines the scales yet; Score combines Probability with the highest of the four Impact ratings, the relative scoring of Book of Forms form 2.32, PDF page 140 (printed page 129). Owner proposes a role, since no document names a risk owner. Responsible Party is the Responsible Person of the work packages in Actions, and Actions are the packages that the R1 to R12 responses row of the coverage check in Part 2 of `docs/scope-package.en.md` assigns to each response, under the work order numbering; a risk the row does not list has empty Actions.*

*The revised columns of the second bank stay empty until a response is carried out. Status is Open for every risk because no document records a response as complete. The strategy in Comments is the preparer's reading, not the charter's. Two change requests (form 3.3) are pending: the schedule change request of 30 September 2026 lists R8 and R9 as updated, and the scope change request of 8 October 2026 raises R6 and defers F12. Every risk text follows the charter until the sponsor decides; Comments say what each request would change.*
