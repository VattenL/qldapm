# 1.3 Stakeholder Register

*Form 1.3, A Project Manager's Book of Forms, 3rd edition, PDF page 25 (printed page 14), filled as the output of PMBOK 6 section 13.1.3.1, printed page 514 (PDF page 553). Built from the charter as committed on 16 September 2026 and amended on 14 November 2026 (Key stakeholder list, Users and roles, assumptions, approval requirements, budget, and exit criteria), form 2.28 Resource Requirements, and form 2.32 Risk Register version 1.1. The twenty rows match the twenty rows of the charter's Key stakeholder list. Eight of them were added to that list on 14 November 2026, each from a named place in the project's documents: S03 from Name and authority of the sponsor, which says the sponsor champions the project with the branch managers, and from assumption 2, which counts them among the 3 academic managers; S08 from Users and roles and risk R15; S13 from the project manager's staffing authority; S14 from exit criterion 7; S17 and S18 from risk R12 and budget line 2; S19 from budget line 2 and acceptance criterion A12; and S20 from acceptance criterion A04 and risk R13.*

*The ID column is not printed on the form; it is added so that each row of this register and of form 1.4 Stakeholder Analysis can be matched. Position/Role gives the position first and then the role on the project. Contact Information is empty where no project document holds the stakeholder's contact details; it is completed when the team has them. Requirements are the stakeholder's high-level needs of the project or the product, and Expectations what the stakeholder expects of the way the project runs; for an external supplier or authority, Requirements are the terms it sets. Classification uses three of the models of PMBOK 6 section 13.1.3.1: internal or external, where internal means the center or the supplier that performs the project; the direction of influence of PMBOK 6 section 13.1.2.4, printed page 513 (PDF page 552); and the quadrant of the interest and influence grid of form 1.4. The last two are the preparer's reading and are highlighted.*

*The scope change request of 8 October 2026 (form 3.3) is pending and would defer F12. If the sponsor approves it, MOB1 leaves S12, the Head Teacher's sign-off of F12 leaves S07, and S17 to S19 have no work in this project. The register follows the charter until the sponsor decides.*
