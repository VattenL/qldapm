| **Detailed Description of Proposed Change** | Re-baseline the milestones to the resource-levelled schedule of form 2.18: M1 from Fri 2 October 2026 to <mark>Wed 28 October 2026</mark>; M2 from Fri 16 October 2026 to <mark>Thu 12 November 2026</mark>; M3 from Fri 13 November 2026 to <mark>Thu 10 December 2026</mark>; M4 from Fri 11 December 2026 to <mark>Tue 12 January 2027</mark>; M5 from Fri 18 December 2026 to <mark>Fri 5 February 2027</mark>; M6 go-live from Tue 5 January 2027 to <mark>Tue 16 February 2027</mark>; M7 closeout from Fri 5 February 2027 to <mark>Fri 19 March 2027</mark>. M0 is unchanged. Keep the five full-time staff for the extra <mark>30 working days</mark> this adds, and increase the personnel budget line to fund them. No activity, estimate, function or acceptance criterion changes. |
| **Justification for Proposed Change** | The charter dates cannot be staffed. Spread over their dictionary dates, the estimates put a person above 40 hours in 32 person-weeks, the worst being QA1 at 276 hours in the week of Mon 14 December 2026 and the project manager at 124 hours in the week of Mon 28 September 2026. Resource leveling (PMBOK 6 section 6.5.2.3) places every one of the 4,400 estimated hours inside a 40-hour week, dividing 93 activities between two people and fast tracking each phase by up to 5 working days (6.5.2.6), and the end date still moves by 30 working days. The binding constraint is the project manager, who is also the business analyst: kickoff, the project management plan and at least half of the requirements work put about 260 hours before M1, which is risk R9. Charter risk R8 requires escalation to the sponsor on any forecast slip of go-live past 15 January 2027; this request is that escalation. |
