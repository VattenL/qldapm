# 2.28 Resource Requirements

*Form 2.28, A Project Manager's Book of Forms, 3rd edition, PDF page 126 (printed page 115); PMBOK 6 section 9.2.3.1, printed page 325 (PDF page 364). Built from the charter as committed on 16 September 2026 and the scope package as committed on 14 October 2026. Team rows are the dictionary's resource codes with their hours; physical rows are the dictionary activities that buy something, keyed by activity ID. The form has no work package column, so a work package is named in Assumptions or Comments, and the basis of estimate sits in Comments with no column added. Software, accounts, and services are written as supplies, since the form has no software kind. A physical quantity is the dictionary's unit count with its amount, because the dictionary prices each item as one unit; counts of laptops or licences are not stated.*

*The pending change requests are not applied. `3-3-change-request-scope.en.md` defers F12, which removes MOB1 and the rows 1.6.1.2-A3 and 1.9.1.1-A4; `3-3-change-request.en.md` moves the dates and keeps these quantities. The charter and the scope package stay the baseline until the sponsor decides.*
