# 2.28 Resource Requirements

*Form 2.28, A Project Manager's Book of Forms, 3rd edition, PDF page 126 (printed page 115); PMBOK 6 section 9.2.3.1, printed page 325 (PDF page 364). Built from the charter as committed on 16 September 2026, the scope package as committed on 14 October 2026, and the cost estimate of 8 October 2026, form 2.20, which this form follows row for row. Team rows give the role, the skills the work needs, the experience and degree, and the hours booked on the project; physical rows give the item, its minimum specification, the quantity, and the amount. The form has no work package or skill column, so skills sit under Resource, experience and degree under Assumptions, and the work packages and the basis under Comments. Software, accounts, and services are written as supplies, since the form has no software kind. Highlighted content is a choice or a specification that no source confirms, for the team to confirm.*

*The reduced scope of form 2.20 is applied here, ahead of the sponsor's decision on the scope change request that carries it: F07, F08, and F12 are deferred, and the supplier's company rules decide what is not a project cost. Against the version of 20 October 2026, this removes the mobile developer, the store accounts, and the test phones, which leave with F12; the external data-entry support, because the center enters and cleans its own source data (risk R6); the team workspace and the laptops, because the team works remotely on its own machines (company rules 1 and 2); the development tools allowance, replaced by GitHub Free and one Figma seat (company rule 3); the gateway credit, because the center provides and pays for its gateway account (assumption 12); and documentation printing, because manuals are delivered as files (company rule 4). The team rows cost 587,661,000 VND and the physical rows 30,348,000 VND, the Labor Costs and Physical Costs totals of form 2.20.*
